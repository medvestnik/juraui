Re: Q2 dashboard feedback

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for sharing the updated dashboard mockups — the team has been going through them and overall the feedback has been very positive. The new analytics section in particular looks great, and the side-by-side chart layout is much cleaner than the old stacked approach.

One thing I noticed is that the bounce rate card could use a bit more context. Right now it just shows 38.4% but it's not immediately clear whether that's good or bad for our industry. Would it be possible to add a small benchmark indicator or a tooltip explaining the ideal range? Even a simple colored badge like you used on the revenue card would help.

Also, the traffic sources table is excellent. The progress bar visualization makes it immediately scannable — much better than the pie chart we had before. Priya mentioned the same thing in our standup this morning.

A few smaller notes from the rest of the team:

  • The "Export" button in the topbar — can it export all charts together as a PDF, or just the data as CSV?
  • The date range selector (7d / 30d / 90d) is perfect. Can we add a "Custom" option in a future iteration?
  • Mobile layout looks great in the screenshots — nice work on the responsive breakpoints.

Overall this is shaping up really nicely for the Q2 release. Let me know if you'd like to set up a quick call to walk through the remaining open items — I'm free Tuesday afternoon or Thursday morning.

Best,
Marcus
